03 · Featured Project

The biggest thing I'm building.

Live product · Oct 2026 – Present

Wakel

A local AI agent that runs real tasks in your apps, terminal and WhatsApp, on desktop and from your phone.

You tell Wakel what you need in plain words, like "resize these photos and email them to the client". It plans the steps, works inside your real apps, and reports back as it goes. Anything sensitive, such as deleting files, publishing a post or sending an email, waits for your approval first.

I built the Wakel mobile app, your remote for the agent, and its AI agent. I also own the Windows version of the desktop agent, ported from macOS with the core developer.

Smart Contract Assistant

View on GitHub

A RAG assistant that answers questions about contract documents using LLMs and LangChain. Upload a contract, ask in plain language, and get answers grounded in the actual clauses.

  • Document chunking and embeddings stored in a FAISS index
  • Retrieval with LangChain feeding the LLM only relevant clauses
  • Guardrails to keep answers on-topic and grounded
  • Evaluation of answer quality, with a Gradio interface
